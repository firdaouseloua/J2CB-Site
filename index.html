01 · About

Built by students, designed for scientific exchange.

JC2B is a student-organised conference that aims to highlight the work of young scientists, strengthen links between training programmes, foster intergenerational exchange, encourage collaboration between teams and open networking opportunities in academia and industry.

The 2025 edition featured a programme, abstracts, registration, call for contributions, practical information, organization and contact sections; this version keeps that structure but presents it in a more modern and compact way.

02 · Highlights

What JC2B is here to do.

Present
